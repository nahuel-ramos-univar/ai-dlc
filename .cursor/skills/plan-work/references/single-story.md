# Single Story

Ask about the user outcome, primary actor, observable success, exclusions, and open business questions. Do not ask implementation, schema, deployment, or test-runner questions.

Propose:

- title;
- business narrative;
- acceptance criteria expressed as observable behavior;
- exclusions;
- assumptions and technical constraints, clearly labeled;
- duplicate candidates and rationale.

Technical constraints should explain the impact in business language. Hand implementation details to `refine-story`.
