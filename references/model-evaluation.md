# Model and generative evaluation

Load this reference only when the authorized change affects the behavior of
a machine-learning model or a generative system. Do not treat it as a
project-wide flag. Discover and reuse tools and datasets the repository
already has. Do not impose a cloud vendor, an evaluation library, a
second-model judge, or a folder layout.

Authorized evaluations may run. External calls and billed work need
authorization before they start.

## Who owns what

- `/plan-work` records the expected outcome, unacceptable failures, and
  agreed success criteria in business language.
- `/refine-story` records the evaluation dataset, rubric, metrics,
  thresholds, configuration, and evaluation budget when those are approved.
- `/implement-change` keeps deterministic tests for surrounding code, and
  evaluations for model behavior.
- `/validate-change` reviews that the evaluation ran, the results, any
  regression, and the limits of what was shown.

## Evaluation contract

Cover representative cases, boundary cases, and adverse cases. Use an
expected response or a rubric, whichever fits the problem. Record
provenance and review of examples: data generated by AI is not
automatically a golden set. Version the dataset, model, prompts, and
configuration. Keep examples used to adjust the system separate from cases
reserved for evaluation.

Use only approved metrics and thresholds. Do not invent them to get a green
result. Report per-case and aggregate results. When variability matters,
repeat the run; a seed does not guarantee determinism. If another model
judges the output, record that judge's limits and calibration.

External calls and their cost need authorization. Do not start them from
this reference alone. Mocks may prove parsing, errors, and integration
contracts. They do not prove the quality of the model they replaced.

Sanitized fixtures and evaluators may live in Git. Sensitive or large
datasets stay in appropriate storage; version the references. Jira keeps
approved criteria and a summary or links to results, not a mandatory copy
of the dataset.
